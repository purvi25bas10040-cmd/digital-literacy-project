🔹 Prevention Checklist (Stay Safe Online)
Always check the sender’s email address before clicking any link
Do not share OTP, passwords, or bank details with anyone
Avoid clicking on suspicious or unknown links
Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts
Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible
Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking or payments
Double-check UPI IDs before sending money
Never approve unknown payment requests on UPI apps
Regularly update your apps and devices for security
Report any cyber fraud immediately on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
